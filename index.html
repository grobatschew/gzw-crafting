Disassembly materials: raw versus disassembled

Raw is the best price any vendor pays for the material itself. Disassembled is the sum of the best vendor price for each component it breaks down into (assuming one of each component per disassembly). A component without a known price is left out, so that total is then a minimum.

Prices are per single item as the game shows them. Items with uses left (kits, bandages, packs) are priced by uses left, marked with an asterisk. K amounts are as displayed, for example $2.5K.
